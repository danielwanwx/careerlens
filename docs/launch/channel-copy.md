## LinkedIn

Career advice becomes dangerous when facts and assumptions look identical.

I built CareerLens to keep four states separate: proved, claimed, missing, and
unknown. It produces an auditable diagnosis, targeted interview guidance, and
a preparation plan that fits a real time budget—without inventing experience.

The new public AI-engineering case is reproducible: licensed fictional
candidate, dated official role evidence, canonical JSON, deterministic HTML,
and strict validation. The result is conditional, with no numeric fit score.
