# Launch measurement template

Record observations by UTC date and channel. Stars are a weak attention signal;
the stronger signals are completed case views, successful installs, useful
issues, repeat users, and contributions.

## Qualitative review

- What promise caused people to click?
- Where did installation or understanding fail?
- Which evidence-state decision was challenged?
- Did readers understand that the candidate is fictional?
- Which question appeared at least three times?
- What should be fixed before the next channel?

## Decision rule

Continue promotion only if trust defects are bounded and at least one strong
signal improves: successful install, completed case inspection, reproducible
issue, repeat use, or contribution. Do not optimize solely for stars.
