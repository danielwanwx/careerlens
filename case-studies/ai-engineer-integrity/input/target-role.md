## Role purpose

Build and deploy machine-learning models and classifiers that improve platform
integrity, content understanding, abuse prevention, and operational stability.
The work connects research, production engineering, model quality, data
pipelines, deployment, monitoring, and cross-functional product decisions.

## Requirements used by this case

1. Design and deploy production machine-learning models.
2. Demonstrate deep-learning and transformer experience.
3. Build scalable data pipelines and optimize model performance and accuracy.
4. Monitor and maintain deployed models.
5. Use PyTorch or TensorFlow.
6. Apply data structures, algorithms, and software-engineering principles.
7. Understand LLM training or fine-tuning methods, including distillation,
   supervised fine-tuning, and policy optimization.
8. Content understanding or abuse prevention with LLMs is a plus.
9. Collaborate across research, engineering, and product.
10. Own ambiguous problems end to end and move quickly among competing
    priorities.
