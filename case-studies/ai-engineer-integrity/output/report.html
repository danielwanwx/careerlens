Resume guidance with truth boundaries

The goal is not to manufacture a closer match. It is to strengthen what can be defended and leave genuine gaps explicit.

Priority A

Integrity Domain Gap

The target team protects the platform from content abuse and scaled attacks; general RAG and recommendation work does not prove that judgment.

Safe direction
Add only candidate-confirmed integrity, trust-and-safety, fraud, anomaly, abuse, or adversarial evaluation work. If none exists, leave it as a preparation gap.
Truth boundary
Do not relabel generic anomaly detection as abuse prevention without matching users, threats, labels, and decisions.
Done when
A reviewer can identify the threat model, data, model decision, failure cost, and measured result.
Priority A

Post Training Proof

The posting names distillation, supervised fine-tuning, and policy optimization, while the resume says only 'fine-tuning techniques.'

Safe direction
Replace the broad phrase with a candidate-confirmed method, dataset construction, objective, evaluation protocol, and trade-off, or remove the implication of depth.
Truth boundary
Do not infer SFT, RLHF, DPO, distillation, or policy optimization from the generic fine-tuning phrase.
Done when
The candidate can defend the exact post-training method and explain why it was selected.
Priority B

Ownership Clarity

Senior interviewers will separate platform scope from the candidate's personal technical decisions.

Safe direction
For SmartAssist, identify one architecture decision, one failed approach, one model-quality trade-off, and the candidate's direct ownership.
Truth boundary
Keep team outcomes and individual contributions distinct.
Done when
The answer names the candidate's decision, alternatives, evidence, and consequence without expanding ownership.

Read less. Master the right parts.

Four non-duplicative sources with reading scope and mastery checks.

  1. 1
    OpenAI model optimization and fine-tuning guide

    Read: Study when to fine-tune, data formatting, holdout evaluation, and iteration boundaries.

    Master: Compare prompting, retrieval, SFT, and distillation for one integrity classifier scenario.

  2. 2
    Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback

    Read: Read the method, data-collection, reward-model, PPO, and evaluation sections; capture the pipeline and failure modes.

    Master: Draw the SFT-to-reward-model-to-policy-optimization flow and name two sources of bias or reward misspecification.

  3. 1
    OpenAI safety evaluations hub

    Read: Study evaluation categories, measurement boundaries, and how results are communicated.

    Master: Define an abuse-evaluation suite with offline quality, adversarial coverage, and production monitoring layers.

  4. 2
    OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications

    Read: Focus on prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, excessive agency, and unbounded consumption.

    Master: Map four threats to signals, mitigations, residual risks, and measurable alarms.

  5. 1
    Google Rules of Machine Learning

    Read: Read rules 1–12, 21–28, and 32–39; focus on pipelines, first models, training-serving skew, feedback loops, and launch criteria.

    Master: Explain how data, training, serving, monitoring, and retraining fail independently and name one guardrail for each.

  6. 2
    AWS Builders' Library: Using load shedding to avoid overload

    Read: Read the sections on overload detection, request prioritization, load shedding, retry amplification, and testing.

    Master: Defend when the classifier path sheds, queues, degrades, or fails closed under three overload scenarios.

  7. 1
    OpenAI evaluation best practices

    Read: Read eval design, dataset construction, grader choice, continuous evaluation, and failure analysis examples.

    Master: Implement the harness with deterministic tests and explain grader disagreement, leakage, and regression thresholds.

  8. 2
    LeetCode Top Interview 150

    Read: Use only hash map, sliding window, heap, intervals, graph traversal, and design-a-data-structure sections; complete one new and one spaced-repeat problem per session.

    Master: Solve two unseen medium variants in 35 minutes each with clarification, tests, and complexity analysis.
