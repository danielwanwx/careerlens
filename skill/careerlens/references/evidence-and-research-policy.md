# Evidence and research policy

Use this source precedence:

1. candidate-owned artifacts for personal facts and outcomes;
2. official jobs, company guidance, government sources, and primary technical
   documentation for target or learning claims;
3. universities and established learning platforms;
4. community reports for discovery or calibration only.

Classify claims as `fact`, `inference`, `community_signal`, or `unknown`.
Classify sources as `candidate_owned`, `official`, `primary_technical`,
`government`, `university`, `established_learning`, `community`, or `unknown`.

Jobs, compensation, benefits, interview process, immigration, sponsorship,
and market conditions are volatile. Store a URL, freshness state, confidence,
and limitation. A failed lookup is an infrastructure result, not proof that a
role or claim does not exist.

Recommend a resource only when it names the linked gap, exact reading scope,
what to skip, sequence, dependencies, and a measurable completion condition.
